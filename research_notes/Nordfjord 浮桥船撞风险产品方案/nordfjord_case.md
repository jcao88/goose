## 1. Site geometry: fjord, depth, shoreline, ferry route, weather

### Takeaway
Nordfjorden is 106 km long and up to 565 m deep. The deepest water lies near the mouth of Eidsfjorden and off Bryggja, which is close to the middle-fjord crossing area. The two Anda–Lote ferry landings are about 2 km apart in a straight line. The ferry crossing takes about 11 minutes, with departures roughly every 20 minutes (about 53–55 per day each way). In 2025 it carried 513,519 vehicles, about 1,400 per day. I found no sourced depth profile along the crossing line and no wind or fog statistics.

### Inferences
- Lote is on the main fjord, south and east of Nordfjordeid. If Hamnes (the Eidsfjorden split) lies west of the Anda–Lote line, as the descriptions suggest, ships bound for Nordfjordeid would not cross the line. Ships to the inner fjord (Utvik, Innvik, Olden, Loen, Stryn) must cross it. Whether Sandane traffic crosses depends on which side of the Gloppefjorden mouth Anda lies. Confirm all of this on a nautical chart before building the scene.
- Because the deepest water (565 m) is "near the mouth of Eidsfjorden", the crossing line probably runs over very deep water, likely hundreds of metres. That points toward a floating bridge without side mooring, like Bergsøysund and Nordhordland, rather than piers. This is an inference, not a measured profile.
- For the parametric model: the straight-line span is about 2.0 km landing to landing. The ferry track is a little longer; the "2.3–2.5 km" figure in the brief was not confirmed by any source.

## 2. Bridge concepts, studies, NTP status and politics

### Takeaway
Nordfjordbrua AS, owned locally, promoted a low-clearance floating bridge across Anda–Lote. Shipping would have passed through a narrow channel blasted through the Andatangen/Andaneset headland, crossed by a bascule (lifting) bridge; an alternative concept used a lock. In July 2024 the Ministry of Transport ordered a study. Kystverket answered on 8 December 2024: 75 m air clearance, 200 m free width and 20 m depth, and a "no" to the floating bridge plus blasted channel. Statens vegvesen advised against the bascule solution in 2025, and the Ministry shelved the project, approximately in early 2026. The official E39 line remains the inner crossing at Svarstad–Frøholm in Stryn (a suspension bridge with a 1,555 m main span), which is not prioritised in NTP 2025–2036.

### Inferences
- The case pits a deliberately low-cost, low-air-draft floating bridge against a national main fairway with deep-sea cruise traffic. Kystverket's 75 m × 200 m × 20 m envelope effectively requires a high bridge or a very large opening span. The local concept instead moved the fairway into a short, narrow, blasted side channel under a moving span. Collision risk in that channel under wind (drift and leeway of high-windage cruise ships) was the decisive argument. This makes the case a good test for comparing a "channel-diversion" geometry with a "high navigation span" geometry in a parametric ship-collision model.
- Stryn's opposition is probably driven more by E39 routing (keeping the inner Svarstad–Frøholm line and its funding) than by cruise access alone. The cruise-access argument for Olden and Loen is the technical lever Kystverket used. This is inferred from the "omkamp" coverage; I found no Stryn council resolution text.
- Model parameters: the Kystverket design envelope is width 200 m, air clearance 75 m, depth 20 m. The Nordfjordbrua concept was a floating girder across about 2 km, plus a blasted channel through Andatangen with a bascule span. The channel width that was drawn is unknown, and Statens vegvesen said it was below 200 m.

- I found no source confirming that Stryn municipality formally voted to stop the study; only press reports of strong reaction.

## 3. Navigation and ship traffic across the Anda–Lote line

### Takeaway
Nordfjorden is a designated main fairway ("hovudlei"). All cruise traffic to Olden and Loen at the head of the fjord (Stryn municipality) passes the Anda–Lote line. That was about 150 calls and about 300,000–330,000 passengers a year in 2023–2025, with ships up to Iona size (P&O, several 2026 calls). Nordfjordeid (80 calls in 2025) is in Eidsfjorden and most likely west of the line. Nordfjord is north of Fedje VTS's limit (Sognesjøen), so it appears not to be covered by any coastal VTS. I found no data on pilotage, cargo, well boats or incidents.

## 4. Analogous floating-bridge projects and navigation-clearance practice

### Takeaway
Norway's two operating floating bridges (Bergsøysund 1992, about 933 m; Nordhordland 1994, about 1,246 m floating part, 32 m navigation span) and the Bjørnafjorden design (about 5.4 km, with a 45–55 m clearance channel 400–490 m wide) are the closest analogues. Bjørnafjorden studies give concrete ship-collision design inputs: collision probability above 10⁻⁴ per year, design collision energy of 250 MJ, and 11.5 m girder clearance against cruise forecastles up to 17 m. Hood Canal, Lake Washington, Sulafjorden and Halsafjorden could not be searched.

### Cited Findings
**Bjørnafjorden**
- An early mega-bridge concept study: the fjord has heavy commercial and naval traffic, so the navigation channel must be 400 m wide and 20 m deep with 45 m air clearance. Another concept put the channel between the tower and the first pontoon at 490 m × 55 m — [ResearchGate: «Mega-bridge concepts for Bjørnafjord crossing in Norway»](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286879218_Mega-bridge_concepts_for_Bjornafjord_crossing_in_Norway)
- Norconsult's floating bridge design: total length 5,440 m, floating main span 5,130 m, pontoon spacing 120 m, high-bridge (cable-stayed) main span 450 m, ship clearance 50 m — [Norconsult: Bjørnafjorden floating bridge](https://norconsult.com/projects/bjoernafjorden-floating-bridge/). Structurae lists the crossing with a date of 2033 — [Structurae](https://structurae.net/en/structures/bjornafjord-crossing). Statens vegvesen has continued developing the floating-bridge solution — [NTB/SVV: «Videreutvikler flytebruløsningen over Bjørnafjorden»](https://kommunikasjon.ntb.no/pressemelding/17954766/videreutvikler-flytebrulosningen-over-bjornafjorden?publisherId=17847490)
- Ship-collision design: a risk analysis found collision probability above 10⁻⁴ per year, so collision could not be excluded from design. Continuous-span girder clearance was 11.5 m while cruise forecastle decks reach about 17 m, so a cruise bow can hit the girder directly. The floating bridge was shown to withstand the proposed 250 MJ collision energy — [Procedia Engineering 2017: «Dynamic responses of a floating bridge subjected to ship collision load on bridge girders»](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877705817339176/pdf?md5=90677b73675674ae9f057306b7c7173c&pid=1-s2.0-S1877705817339176-main.pdf); [ResearchGate version](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/319654389_Dynamic_responses_of_a_floating_bridge_subjected_to_ship_collision_load_on_bridge_girders). Related papers: [OMAE2017 «Ship Collision Analysis of a Floating Bridge in Ferry-Free E39 Project»](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/OMAE/proceedings-abstract/OMAE2017/57779/V009T12A021/279310); [«Design of floating bridge girders against accidental ship collision loads»](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/308614736_Design_of_floating_bridge_girders_against_accidental_ship_collision_loads); [ISOPE 2020: shipping container impact with bridge girders](https://onepetro.org/ISOPEIOPEC/proceedings-abstract/ISOPE20/All-ISOPE20/ISOPE-I-20-3227/446663)
- A local opinion piece puts the Bjørnafjorden floating bridge at NOK 20–30 billion (secondary, unverified) — [Fjordingen: «Nordfjordbrua – langt fram»](https://www.fjordingen.no/meiningar/i/gwaPQB/nordfjordbrua-langt-fram)

**Nordhordland bridge (1994)**
- A floating section of about 1,246 m (steel box girder on 10 pontoons, not laterally anchored because of the water depth) joins a cable-stayed fixed bridge over the sailing channel. The 172 m main span hangs from a single 99 m H-pylon and gives 32 m clearance. One end is anchored directly to shore, the other to a caisson set into rock at about 30 m depth — [Wikipedia: Nordhordland Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordhordland_Bridge); [Aas-Jakobsen](https://www.aas-jakobsen.com/project/nordhordlandbridge-2/)

**Bergsøysund bridge (1992)**
- Bergsøysund floating bridge, completed 1992, length 933 m (3,061 ft) — [Wikipedia: List of pontoon bridges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_pontoon_bridges)

**Clearance practice (other Norwegian fjords)**
- Hardangerbrua has a 55 m sailing height — [Statens vegvesen: Hardangerbrua](https://www.vegvesen.no/vegprosjekter/fullfort/hardangerbrua/brua/)
- Romsdalsaksen (Møre og Romsdal, not Nordfjord): in a 2019 Statens vegvesen study, 75 m clearance was presented publicly as a firm Kystverket requirement, while internally the cost at 45 m was being explored — [Tidens Krav](https://www.tk.no/utad-seilingshoyden-ma-vare-75-meter-innad-hva-skjer-hvis-vi-far-ga-ned-til-45-meter/s/5-51-1030819)
- Nordfjord: Kystverket requires 75 m / 200 m / 20 m — [Fjordabladet](https://www.fjordabladet.no/nyhende/i/EyEW93/skip-ville-mest-sannsynleg-gaatt-paa-grunn). Bjørnafjorden used 45–55 m — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/286879218_Mega-bridge_concepts_for_Bjornafjord_crossing_in_Norway); [Norconsult](https://norconsult.com/projects/bjoernafjorden-floating-bridge/)

### Inferences
- Clearance demands scale with the traffic: 32 m at Nordhordland (local and coastal traffic), 45–55 m at Bjørnafjorden (commercial and naval), and 75 m in Nordfjord (Kystverket's full cruise envelope). In Norway, cruise traffic appears to push Kystverket to the 75 m envelope that tall modern cruise ships need.
- The Bjørnafjorden collision method can be reused for Anda–Lote with a cruise-dominated design fleet: an annual collision frequency threshold of 10⁻⁴, a design energy of about 250 MJ, and a bow/forecastle height above the girder. Here the girder-height mismatch is the critical geometric parameter.
